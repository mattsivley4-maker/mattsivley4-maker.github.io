GMR data + sources

Scope

GMR: Air Evac Lifeteam, Med-Trans programs, AirMed International and Guardian Flight in Texas and within 75 NM of its border. This research page also includes the selected HALO, PHI and Air Methods locations described below; those operators are not GMR. This is not a complete statewide inventory of PHI or every Air Methods program. Co-located AeroCare I / III in Lubbock are one site. AirMed International and AEL 148 retain separate records and radii at San Antonio International Airport.

Accuracy Limits

Circles are WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesics. Eligibility uses the nearest point on the Census 2024 1:500,000 Texas boundary. Address and campus markers are approximate, not surveyed landing pads. Near-threshold sites require precise-coordinate confirmation. Seven user-provided roster excerpts were cross-checked October 20, 2026; their effective date and completeness are not established. Phones are transcribed as supplied, not independently dial-verified. Missing roster entries do not establish closure.

Hospitals — November 3, 2026 review

348 hospital locations: 22 Level I, 31 Level II, 62 Level III, 174 Level IV, and 59 local hospitals without a current listed trauma designation. Flags use the same projection as the air-base map. Green = I, yellow = II, purple = III, blue = IV, gray = no designation listed. Level is assigned to the specific facility, never inherited from its hospital system.

Trauma levels and contingent/probationary notes follow the Texas DSHS list dated October 28, 2026. Hospital addresses follow the October 21 HHSC general/special directory and psychiatric directory, supplemented by official military, VA, state and university hospital pages. Statewide trauma addresses also use the HHSC limited-services rural directory.

Scope: all Texas Level I–IV entries on the state list. The 59 gray hospitals retain the original scope: the 12 highlighted counties plus their neighbors, including corner contacts (33 counties); they are not a statewide non-trauma inventory. Outpatient-only clinics and freestanding ERs are excluded; licensed ER-and-inpatient hospitals are included. University main-campus services, including the Women's & Children's Hospital, share one record. Separate licensed facilities at the same point remain selectable.

Statewide trauma and local gray-hospital scope

Location precision: 295 current addresses match the public HHSC location layer, 52 use geocoded published addresses, and one uses coordinates published by its operator. All 348 points were checked against their mapped Texas county. These are approximate address/campus locations, not surveyed entrances or landing pads. Review limits: this is a dated directory snapshot, not proof of current admissions, emergency capability or transport acceptance. Gray does not imply an emergency department. HALO, nearby PHI and AirLIFE TEXAS — October 27, 2026

HALO: purplePHI: yellowAir Methods: brown

11 publicly supported regional sites and one unresolved launch-site candidate. Select a name below for its address, coordinate precision, evidence and limitations. Every displayed circle uses the same 75 NM (138,900 m) geodesic radius. The map-only and Ingram pages are separate.

HALO has three named locations. Alice is in Jim Wells County, Beeville is in Bee County, and Corpus Christi is in Nueces County. Alice and Jim Wells are not two bases. HALO identifies the Alice and Beeville hospital campuses; FAA-derived heliport records supply estimated pad coordinates. Its two official pages describe Corpus Christi's response/support role differently, so that site's role is flagged. HALO bases · HALO fleet roles

Nearby PHI screening: the public PHI locator was screened for centers within 150 NM of a HALO site, because two 75 NM circles can overlap at that distance. Six communities qualify. Victoria is the closest supported general air-medical site, 46.62 NM from HALO Beeville. San Antonio's hospital proxy is 72.99 NM away but its current launch location is unresolved and hidden by default. Columbus, Bay City, Cedar Creek and Richmond also have overlapping circles. These distances do not establish dispatch availability, pediatric/adult capability or membership eligibility. PHI locator · October 2026 community list

Address discrepancies are retained in the evidence. Uvalde AirLIFE uses 403 Airport Blvd, beside the existing AEL 181 address at 399; their coordinates and circles remain distinct. Each Uvalde pop-up links to the other base record.
