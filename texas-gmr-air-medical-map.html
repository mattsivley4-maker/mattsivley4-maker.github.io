Scope

Air Evac Lifeteam, Med-Trans programs, AirMed International and Guardian Flight. Includes Texas bases and out-of-state sites within 75 NM of Texas, regardless of state name. Ground-only operations, offices and non-GMR programs are excluded. Co-located AeroCare I / III in Lubbock are one site. AirMed International and AEL 148 retain separate records and radii at San Antonio International Airport.

Accuracy Limits

Circles are WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesics. Eligibility uses the nearest point on the Census 2024 1:500,000 Texas boundary. Address and campus markers are approximate, not surveyed landing pads. Near-threshold sites require precise-coordinate confirmation. Seven user-provided roster excerpts were cross-checked October 20, 2026; their effective date and completeness are not established. Phones are transcribed as supplied, not independently dial-verified. Missing roster entries do not establish closure.

AEL 47 La Grange is excluded. The owner confirmed internally that this base no longer exists; stale public listings do not override that correction.
